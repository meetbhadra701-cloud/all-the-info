## Remaining unresolved questions

- Can PPA-Delta’s coupled reducer retain a meaningful advantage under stronger, frozen, externally reproducible baselines and a stated effect-size bar?
- Can the memory semantic-contract interaction be formulated as a theorem with a clean independent semantic and physical oracle?
- Can a cross-stage security/semantic guarantee be carried through scheduling and lowering with a genuinely new theorem and artifact?
- Can the Yosys arithmetic-tree defect be reproduced from a clean build and characterized across versions without overclaiming novelty?
