# SEARCHING pass 2 — timing, compiler, security, and boundary checks

Completed September 12, 2026. Private research notes; no implementation or experiments. These continue the interrupted pass. The compiler/security agent's intermediate findings were retained; its session could not be resumed, so the root researcher completed the specific source checks locally. This file does not claim that the unavailable agent's complete raw tool history was recovered.

The previous pass remains an exclusion set. The judgments below reject particular proposed mechanisms, not entire research fields. “Baseline” means the strongest directly relevant method identified for the stated subproblem; it does not imply a universal ranking of incomparable tools.

**1. Collapse timing-event permutations through exact or approximate commutation — KILL.**

The 2025 symbolic timing work explicitly required a supplied internal transition order. Its August 2026 successor constructs those orders, propagates feasibility constraints, and discusses partial-order reduction. The latter still fixes the external input ordering; its prototype evaluation uses the small c17_slack circuit and a constant-delay model. Therefore neither industrial scalability nor exhaustive transistor-accurate verification is established. The old manual-order limitation is nevertheless no longer an unoccupied mechanism. [2025 paper, §IV](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.15907v1), [2026 paper, §§IV–VI and Appendix A](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.04036v1)

The exact wall is that distinct event orders can change downstream timing through drafting, competing inputs, and pulse cancellation. Enumerating equivalent orders is avoidable redundancy; distinguishing physically different orders is a real semantic obligation. No universal lower bound follows merely from one tree-based implementation.

The proposed operation examined was stronger than ordinary pruning: prove that exchanging two events changes the analog/timing state by at most epsilon, merge their permutations, and propagate a certified bound through later events. This would preserve a reachable timing envelope without exploring every order.

The domain-stripped operation already appears in Fan, Huang, and Mitra's *Approximate Partial Order Reduction*: epsilon-independent actions, equivalent sequences formed by swaps, continuity-based reachable-set enclosures, and adjustable precision. It is a 2016 preprint / FM 2018 paper. This is a direct algorithmic threat, not a loose analogy. [Primary paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1610.06317)

The cheapest kill is algebraic: translate the proposed event-swap rule and propagated error recurrence into that framework. Then test its assumptions on a pulse close to a logic threshold. Small voltage or time error does not automatically preserve whether a digital transition exists. A distinct, useful threshold-aware rule was not identified. The would-be reusable object—an error-certified event quotient—therefore remains either established machinery or an unspecified extension. No test was run.

**2. Preserve pulse correlations instead of merging reconvergent glitches — KILL as currently formulated.**

UPSET's 2026 TimeStamp mode explicitly describes potentially exponential pulse-train combinations at reconvergence, then merges same-direction pulse trains. The wall is correlation loss when distinct feasible histories are collapsed. The suggested replacement was a shared symbolic waveform representation with conditional events and composition that retains common causes. [UPSET, §3.4](https://www.mdpi.com/2079-9292/15/4/818)

The current symbolic-timing paper's related-work section identifies older coded time-symbolic simulation using shared BDDs, alongside timed-automata and polyhedral approaches. A decision diagram carrying event conditions is consequently not a new primitive. These older methods do not automatically solve modern history-dependent delay models; their existence rules out the generic representation claim. [Eigner et al., §II and references 15–18](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.04036v1)

The cheapest kill is to write the suggested sharing operation as ordinary decision-diagram apply plus time constraints. A small reconvergent example should distinguish a genuinely new closure rule from merely a different node annotation. Future researchers would need an exact or certified waveform-composition operation; none beyond the existing symbolic operations was specified. The general compactness claim was not established.

**3. Infer schedule/resource interfaces that remain composable — KILL.**

The recovered CGRA lead was verified: Wang et al.'s August 2025 manuscript, subsequently published in 2026, decomposes control/dataflow mapping using liveness information and handles mapping failures with moves or spill/reload operations. Its §7.4 explains why those repairs need not reschedule already solved blocks. The remaining overhead and mapping-quality tradeoffs are real. Merely introducing a live-value boundary or a repair fallback would duplicate existing operations. [CGRA paper, §§6–7](https://arxiv.org/html/2508.02167v1)

MLIR-AIR's 2026 limitations section deliberately leaves some domain decomposition and architecture-specific cost decisions downstream. That is partly a chosen scope boundary; it does not identify a missing mathematical operation. [MLIR-AIR, §10](https://doi.org/10.1145/3785670)

The serious proposed primitive was a resource-and-time contract that can be partially inferred, composed, and solved without reopening every module. Filament already supplies timeline types for static pipelines. A March 23, 2026 LATTE position paper explicitly extends this direction to unspecified timing, binding, and allocation parameters. Its §5 identifies expensive joint SMT solving as a remaining limitation. A position paper establishes a disclosed approach, not a mature performance result. [Filament, PLDI 2023](https://www.cs.cornell.edu/~asampson/media/papers/filament-pldi2023-preprint.pdf), [LATTE 2026 paper](https://capra.cs.cornell.edu/latte26/paper/latte26-final17.pdf)

The exact residual wall is disjunctive resource use coupled with timing and communication choices. Writing the choices as a contract does not remove their search complexity. The cheapest kill is to express the proposed contract elimination in existing difference constraints plus binding/non-overlap choices. If those choices simply return to SMT, constraint programming, or an ILP, the computational mechanism has not changed. No distinct closure rule with a useful hardware scope emerged. The reusable object would be a compositional scheduling contract; both the object and substantial existing constructions already exist.

**4. Track temporal randomness exposure across reused hardware — KILL in its generic form.**

MaskedHLSVerif, March 19, 2026, addresses false positives in HLS-generated controller/datapath circuits through state-wise decomposition and REBECCA. Its stated soundness discussion concerns its assumptions and first-order intermediate-wire leakage. This is evidence against novelty of simple state-sensitive splitting; it does not prove arbitrary HLS optimizations preserve all higher-order or temporal leakage properties. [MaskedHLSVerif, §§V–VII](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.18939v1)

The stronger proposed operation was to export a temporal exposure summary recording which random variables and shares an observer can jointly learn, then compose summaries across register reuse and scheduling. The limitation is fundamental for a summary that retains only per-cycle marginal distributions. For example, observations r and secret XOR r are each uniform for uniform r, but their pair reveals the secret. This is an illustrative mathematical counterexample, not a criticism that the cited tools necessarily use such a weak summary.

MATCHI already tracks share dependencies, randomness, signal validity, and gadget properties during symbolic execution under a glitch-plus-transition probing model. Its compositional strategy uses PINI/OPINI; elementary gadget security and annotations are assumed. Thus “carry randomness provenance and temporal validity through composition” already has a concrete implementation and proof lineage. [Authors' tool and linked foundational papers](https://github.com/cassiersg/matchi)

The cheapest kill is to state the proposed summary's observation quantifiers, then translate its composition rule into that lineage. If it only broadens the accepted netlist syntax, automates annotations, or schedules around known gadget restrictions, it misses the requested bar. Arbitrary sharing remains a difficult problem, but no new inference rule was identified. A compositional exposure certificate could be valuable; the candidate did not supply a distinct one. This concerns power masking and temporal probing, and does not reopen the earlier constant-time HLS projects.

**5. Automatically verify every mixed-level compiler transformation — early exclusion.**

PEQC-MLIR, May 2026, uses a restricted program class and hybrid concrete-symbolic interpretation to obtain equivalence checking linear in executed operations. Its restrictions are essential to the claim. An unrestricted verifier cannot be inferred from it, and adding generic symbolic execution for the unsupported cases is not a new mechanism. The proposed direction also approaches the previous pass's excluded equivalence/proof-reuse families. It was not promoted to a serious survivor. [Primary paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2605.01124)

**Additional boundary checks; these are not proposed projects.**

- **Clock/data joint optimization:** DiffCCD already jointly optimizes clock skew and data delays. Retiming/skew equivalence has a much older algorithmic lineage. Exposing a unified objective or translating skew into register movement does not establish novelty. A new physically realizable multimode algorithm was not specified. [DiffCCD, ICCAD 2025](https://guozz.cn/publication/diffccdiccad-25/diffccdiccad-25.pdf), [Deokar–Sapatnekar lineage](https://www.ece.umn.edu/~sachin/conf/iccd96.pdf)
- **Fast timing propagation:** INSTA already provides differentiable timing propagation with top-K arrivals and common-path pessimism removal. Its paper distinguishes propagation using initialized arc delays from complete timing-tool runtime. “Fast differentiable timing” alone is not an uncovered wall. [Authors' paper and repository](https://github.com/NVlabs/INSTA)
- **NoC/FPGA feasibility:** Congestion-aware placement, turn-model path diversity, and SAT-based rerouting already appear in *The Road Less Traveled*. The manuscript explicitly says it appeared at FPL 2024 although its arXiv upload is August 2026. Its description also acknowledges commercial SAT-based NoC routing. Neither arbitrary exact routability nor all workloads are solved. [Primary paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.17266v1)
- **Approximate-circuit error analysis:** PolyEMAC, VLSID 2026, already combines cutwidth partitioning and ASP for several metrics under constant-cutwidth assumptions. A bounded-width DP in a new application is not itself a new primitive, and no polynomial-width guarantee for arbitrary circuits follows. [Authors' institutional record](https://www.dfki.de/web/forschung/projekte-publikationen/publikation/16594)
- **Approximate synthesis with guaranteed error:** SimALS-MaxError already uses simulation to prune changes and accelerate exact SAT-based maximum-error checks. This is a competitive baseline for its scope, not permission to relabel generate-and-check as a centerpiece. [Author-hosted accepted TCAD 2026 paper](https://si2.epfl.ch/demichel/publications/archive/2026/approx.pdf)

The potentially meaningful shared wall is composition after information has been discarded. However, exact relational composition, dependency tracking, decision-diagram operations, temporal typing, and approximate commutation are already real computational mechanisms. The new pass did not establish an additional operation that changes their limiting behavior. KILL these formulations; retain SEARCHING.
