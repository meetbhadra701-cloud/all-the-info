## E1-hashed: the strong-baseline correction (OBSERVED; `results/E1_hashed.md`)

Per-input trees after structural hashing vs UBP/CBP after hashing (regime F):

| n | p0 = .33: g1h / UBPh | p0 = .50: g1h / UBPh |
|---|---|---|
| 64 | 1.10–1.12 | 1.04–1.05 |
| 128 | 1.17 | 1.03 |
| 256 | 1.17 | 1.07 |
| 512 | 1.195 | 1.089 |
| 1024 | 1.184 | 1.112 |

**Interpretation.**
- A balanced per-input tree built in index order produces partial sums (pairs, quads, …) that recur across rows.
- Standard synthesis merges them automatically. That is the same pigeonhole sharing that UBP makes explicit.
- In full-custom silicon this leaves UBP only 4–20% better, and da4ml is better than both.
- **The pre-registered E1 (F) baseline (unhashed) was therefore weak.** This is the Wave 10 "weak baseline" error, caught by our own gate-level anomaly (E2) and corrected here. **A1 is claimed only for regime (V).** There, base layers cannot depend on W, so no hashing or CSE across rows exists.

## E3: gate level, regime (V) — the test of the thesis (OBSERVED; `results/E3_summary.md`)

**Components:**
- TREE(L, w): L opaque via-selected w-bit leaves;
- GEN(g): all canonical patterns;
- NEG(w): shared negated lines.

Each is synthesized separately, checked independently and by `cec`, and composed with exact counts.

**Iso-delay:** D* = the slowest fabric's critical path. Every tree met its budget exactly. The per-input trees receive the extra slack, so the comparison favours the baseline.

| n = m | per-input g1_V | UBP2 | UBP3 | UBP4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 128 | 4.653 mm² | 3.002 (1.55×) | 2.572 (1.81×) | 2.384 (**1.95×**) |
| 1024 | 303.94 mm² | 184.75 (1.65×) | 147.34 (2.06×) | 121.88 (**2.49×**) |

- **K3 not triggered; ADV3 met.**
- The first attempt (single-expression trees) gave identical n = 128 numbers. It is kept in `results/E3_attempt1_single_expression/` (deviation D1).
- Why the cell ratio (2.49×) is below the adder ratio (3.88×): UBP leaves are 10 bits wide vs 8, and multi-operand adders cost roughly per leaf bit.

## Model-adjusted (V) estimate (MEASURED cells + MODELLED wiring; `results/E3_summary.md`)

Per-site area = max(measured logic per leaf, lines × bits × pitch × 2.72 µm):

| n | g | pitch 0.46 µm | pitch 0.92 µm |
|---|---|---|---|
| 128 | 3 | 1.81× (logic-bound) | 1.81× (logic-bound) |
| 128 | 4 | 1.80× (wire-bound) | 1.00× (wire-bound) |
| 1024 | 3 | **2.06× (logic-bound)** | **2.06× (logic-bound)** |
| 1024 | 4 | 2.24× (wire-bound) | 1.14× (wire-bound) |

**Design rule from this estimate:** g = 3 for bit-parallel fabrics, giving about 2× that grows with n. g = 4 only pays with tight upper-metal pitch. Bit-serial fabrics (1-bit lines) favour g = 3–4 at about 3× (`results/wire_model.txt`, MODEL).
