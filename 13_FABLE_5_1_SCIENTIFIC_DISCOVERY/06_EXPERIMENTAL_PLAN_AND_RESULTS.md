**Reading E1:**

1. **The universality constraint itself is nearly free.**
   - UBP (universal generator) vs CBP (weight-specific block patterns) differ by only 1–4% in U.
   - The residual 1.22–1.37× gap to da4ml is da4ml's *non-block-aligned* sharing (cross-block pairs, shifted terms). No via-programmable fabric can exploit it.
2. **g1/UBP grows with n** (1.78 → 2.12 → 2.40 at p0 = 0.33), as the amortization argument predicts. The optimal g rises from 3 to 4.
3. **da4ml's runtime grows steeply:** about 20× per size doubling (0.5 s → 10 s), and it exceeds the 600 s cap at 256 under load. UBP/CBP constructions run in seconds at 4096 (cost-only).
4. **Pre-registered verdicts:**
   - **K2 not triggered** (median UBP/da4ml = 1.33 < 1.5).
   - **A1's "≤ 1.25" advance clause is not met** (1.33).
   - K1/A1's size clause is evaluated at n ≥ 1024 in 07.

## Analytic wire model (MODEL; `results/wire_model.txt`)

Area gain vs g = 1 in regime (V), with every candidate line physically crossing each site:

| style | pitch 0.46 µm | pitch 0.92 µm |
|---|---|---|
| bit-parallel | best 2.29× at g = 3 | best 1.67× at g = 2–3 |
| bit-serial | best 3.1× at g = 4 | best 3.0× at g = 3 |

At g ≥ 5 every configuration is wire-bound and the gain collapses (0.1–1.3×). **This is a model.** It is the main thing the prototype's PnR must test.
