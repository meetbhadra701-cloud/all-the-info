# Portfolio Evidence Ledger

**Current status:** no external adoption, citation, publication, patent, award or recognition is claimed by this starter pack.

The purpose is to preserve accurate evidence as substantive work occurs. Each agent automatically creates events in its own Evidence folder and links real supporting artifacts. [[Project-Status]] shows recent entries. Do not count task completion or a local second-agent review as independent external validation.

Track: date; specific human/technical contribution; artifact/version; measured result; provenance; external person or organization if actually involved; independent URL or document; adoption/integration; citation; talk/publication/review activity; award/coverage; verification status; limitations; and why the event matters technically. Use [[09-Templates/Evidence-Event]].

Keep immigration records and private identity/legal documents out of public replay bundles. The technical evidence can later support professional assessment; no fixed USCIS counts or approval outcome are asserted. Recognition should follow useful engineering.
